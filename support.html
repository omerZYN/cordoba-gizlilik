Frequently asked questions

What files can I add?

PDF, EPUB and TXT files; documents you scan with the camera; images from your photo library. You can also add a PDF from a link.

Where are my books and notes stored?

Only on your device. Cordoba has no account and no servers; nothing you add ever reaches us.

How do I move my library to a new phone?

On your old device, make a backup with Settings › Backup › “Back up as ZIP” and transfer the file to your new device. On the new device, choose that file with Settings › Backup › “Restore from backup”. Your shelves, files, notes and bookmarks all come along.

Can I get back a book I deleted by mistake?

Yes. Deleted items wait in Settings › Trash for 30 days; you can restore them during that time.

How does translation work?

On Android, the text you select is translated on your device; the first time you use a language, its language pack is downloaded once. On iPhone and iPad, translation opens the text in Google Translate.

How do I remove ads and limits?

Subscribe to Cordoba Premium, monthly or yearly, from Settings › Cordoba Premium. While your subscription is active, the app shows no ads at all, and you can have as many shelves, books, cover colors and cover images as you like. The free version holds up to 5 shelves and 25 books, with 5 cover colors and cover images on up to 5 books.

What happens to my library if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Shelves, books, colors and cover images beyond the free limits stay as they are and you can keep reading and editing them; you just can’t add new ones until you’re back under the limit or subscribe again.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. If you cancel, ads and limits stay off until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I subscribed, but I still see ads.

Open Settings › Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with. Your subscription works on every device that uses that account.

Why does the app ask about tracking or show a consent form?

The free version shows ads from Google. On iPhone and iPad, Apple requires apps to ask before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads; in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your consent. You can decline — the app works the same, but the ads may be less relevant. You can change your choice later in Settings › Ad privacy choices or in your device settings.

Can I see the intro again?

Settings › Help › “Show the intro again”.
